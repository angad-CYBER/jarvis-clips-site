Privacy Policy

Jarvis Clips is a personal tool run by Angad Singh on their own computer to publish short gaming clips to their own social media accounts. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Who uses it

Only the owner. There are no other user accounts, no sign-ups and no public service. The accounts connected to it belong to the owner.

What it collects

When the owner connects an account, the app receives an access token from that platform and the account's id and username. For each clip it publishes, it keeps the post's id, time and status. It does not collect information about viewers, followers or anyone else, and it does not use cookies or analytics on this website.

How it is used and shared

Tokens are used only to publish clips the owner approved, to the account the owner chose, and to show which account is connected. Data is stored on the owner's own computer, not on any server or cloud service, and is never sold or shared with anyone. Videos sent to Instagram are placed briefly in private cloud storage so Instagram can fetch them, and removed within a day.

YouTube API Services

Jarvis Clips uses YouTube API Services to upload videos the owner approved to the owner's channel. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. YouTube data the app receives is kept no longer than 30 days unless refreshed, except the ids of videos it uploaded. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok and Instagram

Connected TikTok and Instagram accounts can be disconnected at any time in the app, which deletes the stored token, and access can also be removed in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites).

Deleting data

Disconnecting an account deletes its token immediately. To ask for deletion of anything else related to an account, write to the contact below; it is deleted within 7 days.
